# Contributing to Alchemy Craft

Thank you for your interest in **Alchemy Craft**!

## Reporting Issues & Feature Ideas

- **Bug Reports:** Please open an issue on [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/elyazaliverdiyev/alchemy-craft/issues) and include your OS, app version, and steps to reproduce.
- **Craft Feature Proposals:** Share your ideas for bookbinding textures, active reading workflows, or acoustic soundscapes in the issue tracker or via Telegram (`@StratAlchemy`).

## Local Development & Testing

1. Clone the repository into your test vault's `.obsidian/plugins/artcraft-reader/` directory.
2. Modify `main.js` and `styles.css`, then reload the app (`Ctrl+R` / `Cmd+R`) to verify changes in both **Live Preview** and **Reading Mode**.
3. Ensure `styles.css` and `main.js` pass automated Scorecard checks before opening a Pull Request.
