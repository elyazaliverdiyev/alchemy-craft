## ✨ What's New in v3.7.0

1. **🕸️ Swiss Bento Graph Studio & Floating HUD:** Transforms Obsidian's built-in Graph View (`graph` and `localgraph`) with a tactile Swiss dot-grid canvas, radar rings, floating preset HUD (`Swiss Bento`, `Constellation`, `Focus Radar`), and 1-click automatic folder/tag color group injection (`01 Sapphire`, `02 Amber`, `03 Emerald`, `04 Amethyst`, `05 Rose`, `06 Sage Green`).
2. **🌍 6 Global Languages Built-In:** Instant localization across the floating dock, settings tab, Graph HUD, and checkout modal for **English (`en`)**, **Русский (`ru`)**, **中文 (`zh`)**, **日本語 (`ja`)**, **Español (`es`)**, and **Deutsch (`de`)**.
3. **💳 Global Checkout Hub (No Boosty):** Seamless support for **US/EU/Global Bank Cards & Apple Pay (via Tribute / Stripe)**, **1-Click Telegram `@CryptoBot`**, **Universal EVM (`0x5df7...e7e2`)** with chain pills for **BNB Chain (BEP20), Arbitrum One, Base, Polygon, Optimism, Ethereum (ERC20), Avalanche**, plus **Bybit UID (`8015849`)**, **Solana**, and **TRON (TRC-20)**.
4. **🎧 Ambient Focus Soundscapes (Zero-Latency WebAudio):** Built-in synthesized background audio during Pomodoro sprints (`Warm Brown Noise`, `Soft Rain on Paper`, and `40Hz Gamma Binaural Focus Drone`) — zero external MP3 files or internet required.
5. **📊 Focus Flow Analytics & 100% Confetti:** Tracks completed Pomodoro sprints, deep work minutes, 100% completed checklists, and daily streaks right inside Settings, with an alchemical particle burst when you finish 100% of a note's tasks.
6. **🛠️ Bug Fixes & Mobile Rescue:**
   - Fixed vertical pillar dock (`⇄`) clipping buttons on the right edge of the screen.
   - Added a tap-to-dismiss `✕` button on the sticky Task Progress Bar and automatic DOM cleanup on `onunload()` so bars never get stuck on iOS/Android.
   - Fixed `clearAllAnnotations()` (`✕` button) so it cleanly unpins all highlighted sentences (`is-pinned` / `data-pinned-color`).
   - Completed full `Midnight Dark Craft Mode` styling for tables, inline titles, callouts, sidebars, and the floating dock.

## 🚀 Installation & Upgrade

1. Copy `main.js`, `styles.css`, and `manifest.json` into your vault's `.obsidian/plugins/artcraft-reader/` folder.
2. Reload Obsidian (`Ctrl+R` / `Cmd+R`) and enable **Alchemy Craft** in **Settings → Community plugins**.
