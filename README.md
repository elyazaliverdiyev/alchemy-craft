## ✨ What's New in v4.6.8

- **📚 100% Flush Book Folio & 4-Sheet Deckled Edges:** The book spine is aligned strictly flush (`margin-left: 0`) against the left sidebar panel, eliminating empty gaps, accompanied by 4 tactile deckled sheets cascading along the right and bottom edges with authentic paper shadow depths.
- **🧭 Brass Astrolabe Reading & Link Compass:** Smoothly rotating brass astrolabe on scroll displaying reading depth (0–100%) and note link metrics, with a quick navigation menu on click.
- **🎗️ Gold-Foil Spine Embossing & Silk Bookmark Ribbon:** Intricate bookbinder saddle-stitching, raised horizontal ribs, and oscillating silk bookmark ribbon along the leather spine.
- **✨ Alchemist UV Secret Ink:** Highlighted text (`==marked text==`) softly illuminates with an ethereal fluorescent glow under the Smart LED Lamp beam or upon hover.
- **☕ One-Click Cappuccino Focus Recipes:** Instant focus presets (`Morning Cappuccino • 25m`, `Rainy Chemex • 45m`, `Night Roastery 40Hz • 60m`) configuring soundscapes, equalizer, Smart Lamp Kelvin & shape, and Pomodoro timer in one touch.
- **🔖 5-Nutrient Margin Bookmarks (`+ Sticker`):** Pin tactile colored paper ribbon stickers (`Proteins — Must Do`, `Carbs — To Ponder`, `Fats — Stories`, `Vitamins — Quotes`, `Fiber — Facts`) to note sections with smooth jump navigation.
- **📖 Two-Column Open Book Spread (`Book Spread`):** Transforms wide reading notes into an elegant two-page book spread with a warm spine divider.
- **🪟 Zero-Overflow Wide Craft Modals (680px):** Polished, responsive modal layouts with full multi-language date & time localization for the Wax Seal Time Capsule.

## 🚀 Installation

1. Copy `main.js`, `manifest.json`, and `styles.css` into `.obsidian/plugins/artcraft-reader/`.
2. Reload the app (`Ctrl+R` / `Cmd+R`) and enable **Alchemy Craft** in Settings.
