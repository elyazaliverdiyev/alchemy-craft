## 01 // The Architecture of Swiss Bento Reading

Most markdown themes suffer from two extremes: **harsh dark mode glare** or **sterile blinding white backgrounds**. 

Alchemy Craft uses archival Swiss typography principles:
1. **Warm Paper Canvas (`#F4F3EF`):** Low-contrast, eye-soothing background designed for 4-8 hour deep reading and research sessions.
2. **Hairline Precision Borders (`#D5D3CC` / `#111111`):** Structural bento boundaries keep thoughts grounded and prevent eye drift.
3. **Excel Sage Green Tables (`#69BD28`):** Data tables inherit clear financial-grade headers with maximum legibility.

Try reading the paragraph below — notice how numbers and key metrics stand out naturally without cluttering your view.

## 04 // Laser Pointer & Freehand Marker Canvas

Presenting notes on Zoom, recording video walkthroughs, or emphasizing a key diagram during brainstorming? 

- **Laser Pointer:** Click `[Laser]` in the dock (or press your hotkey). Move your cursor across the note — a smooth glowing comet tail follows your mouse with pixel-perfect alignment. Press `Esc` to instantly turn it off.
- **Freehand Marker:** Click `[Draw]` in the dock. Draw arrows, circles, or underlines directly over your text. Pick any color from the dock palette. Click `[✕]` in the dock to clear your annotations.

## 05 // Flexible Focus Timer & Pomodoro Dock

The minimalist pill in the bottom-right corner keeps you in flow without taking over your screen:

- **Left-Click:** Start / Pause the current timer.
- **Double-Click:** Reset timer to your selected duration.
- **Right-Click (The Magic Feature):** Opens an instant popup menu with presets:
  - `⚡ 15 min — Sprint Focus`
  - `🍅 25 min — Standard Pomodoro`

When a session completes, a soothing dual-tone chime plays via the Web Audio API without disturbing your environment.

## 06 // AI Watermark & Zero-Width Space Cleaner

When copying text from ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or web articles, invisible tracking characters (zero-width spaces `\u200B`, joiners `\u200D`, and soft hyphens `\u00AD`) often hitchhike into your vault. These break markdown formatting, code blocks, and search indexing.

### Test Block:
The text block below intentionally contains 4 hidden zero-width spaces between words:
> Artificial​Intelligence​is changing the way we​organize knowledge​forever.

1. Select the text above (or leave it unselected to clean the whole note).
2. Click the `[AI Clean]` button in the bottom floating dock.
3. Alchemy Craft will instantly strip all invisible markers, copy the clean text to your clipboard, and show you a notice with the exact count of removed characters!

## 07 // Pro Features & Theme Coexistence

### 🌙 Midnight Dark Craft Mode (Pro)
For late-night reading and coding, Alchemy Craft Pro unlocks **Midnight Dark Craft** — an exclusive Swiss dark aesthetic engineered with deep charcoal contrasts and glowing accents that protect your circadian rhythm.

### 🎨 Does Alchemy Craft work with your existing themes?
**Yes!** Alchemy Craft is a full-featured plugin, not just a static CSS theme:
- If you love the Swiss Bento aesthetic, keep the `[UI]` button active in the dock.
- If you want to use your own theme (e.g., Minimal, AnuPpuccin, Cyberpunk), simply click `[UI]` in the dock to turn off the paper skin. **All core plugin features remain 100% active:** floating dock timer, laser pointer, freehand marker, sentence pinning, and AI cleaner work flawlessly on top of ANY theme!
