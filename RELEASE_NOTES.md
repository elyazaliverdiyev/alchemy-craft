# Alchemy Craft v3.4.0

### ✨ Features
- **📜 Warm Archival Canvas:** Eye-friendly warm paper background (`#F4F3EF`) with crisp 1px Swiss borders.
- **📊 Sage Green Tables:** High-legibility tabular data with `#69BD28` header accents.
- **🏷️ Bento Badge Numbers:** Automatic transformation of numbered lists `(1-4)` and markers `[01]-[04]` into Swiss square badges.
- **🎯 Sentence Focus Mode:** Hover and click to highlight key thoughts with interactive color pinning.
- **🔦 Pixel-Perfect Laser Pointer & Drawing Canvas:** Present thoughts effortlessly with a glowing laser trail and freehand highlighter. Zero cursor offset.
- **⏱️ Flexible Focus Dock & Pomodoro:** Customizable focus intervals (15m, 25m, 45m, 60m, 90m, plus short/long breaks), preset popup menu, and soothing audio chime.
- **🧹 AI Watermark Cleaner (Optional Toggle):** Remove invisible zero-width characters (`\u200B-\u200D`, `\uFEFF`, soft hyphens) and robotic AI artifacts from notes with 1 click.
- **🌐 Bilingual UI:** English by default for global users with optional Russian localization.
- **🌙 Midnight Dark Craft Mode (Pro):** Deep dark aesthetic engineered specifically for late-night focus and coding.
